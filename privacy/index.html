Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This explains what DoorsAway collects when you visit doorsaway.com, create an account, join a waitlist or buy a report, why we collect it, and who else handles it. The short version: we collect what we need to run accounts and deliver reports, and to learn which pages and prices work. We don’t sell your information, and we don’t share it with agents or lenders.

What we collect

WhenWhat
You create an accountYour email address and a password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in readable form). With Google sign-in: the email and basic profile Google shares. We also store the time you signed up and confirmed your email.
You join a waitlistYour email address and the market you asked about.
You buy a reportYour email, the amount, which market and price you saw, and Stripe’s payment reference. Stripe collects your card and billing details; we don’t receive or store your card number.
You answer our surveyYour answers (budget, timeline, whether you own property, an optional note, and whether a call is okay).
You browse the report pagesWhich page and price you saw, whether you clicked the pre-order button, and the ad campaign tags (UTM parameters) in the link you used. These are tied to a random visitor ID that we store in your browser, not to your name.
You use the siteA session cookie and a security (CSRF) cookie so you stay signed in and requests are protected. Our servers also keep standard technical logs such as IP address and browser type.

Why we use it

  • To run your account, verify your email, and let you sign in or reset your password.
  • To process your payment, deliver your report, and handle refunds and support.
  • To understand which pages, prices and ads lead people to buy, so we can decide what to build.
  • To keep the service secure and to prevent abuse and fraud.
  • To contact you about your account or order. We’ll email you about marketing only if you’ve asked to hear from us, and you can opt out at any time.

Who else handles your data

We use these providers to operate DoorsAway. They process data on our behalf under their own terms:

  • Stripe: payments.
  • Resend: sending account and order emails.
  • Railway: running our servers and database.
  • Cloudflare: domain name and email routing. GitHub: hosting the website pages.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Advertising and analytics tools, such as Meta or Google Analytics, may measure visits and ad results if we turn them on. If we do, they may set their own cookies, and we’ll keep this section up to date.

DoorsAway shares servers and a database with RentalInsights, another product run by the same people, while we transition between the two. An account you create on one may therefore also exist on the other. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a sale or transfer of the business.

How long we keep it

We keep account and order records for as long as your account exists and for as long afterward as we need for refunds, accounting and legal reasons. Waitlist, survey and visit data are kept while we’re running and learning from this test, and are deleted or anonymized when we no longer need them.

Cookies and local storage

We use a session cookie and a security cookie for sign-in. We also keep a random visitor ID, the price you were shown, and the ad tags from your link in your browser’s local storage so you see consistent pricing and we can measure results. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings; if you do, you may be shown a different price.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, including your account, waitlist entry and survey answers. If you’re in a place with privacy laws such as California or the EU/UK, you may have additional rights, and we’ll honor them. We’ll answer within a reasonable time. Records we must keep for payments and taxes may be retained.

Security and children

Passwords are hashed, traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, and sessions use secure cookies. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password. DoorsAway is for adults; we don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above and, for material changes, tell account holders by email where we reasonably can.

Contact

For privacy requests: use the Contact link in the site footer once it appears, or reply to a DoorsAway email from a reachable address.
