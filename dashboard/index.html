      How to read these numbers. Values and rents are estimates, shown with the number of homes (n) behind each one. ◌ Thin data means fewer than 100 homes or fewer than 10 sales in 12 months. ⚠ Check carefully marks gross yields above 15%: estimates are least reliable in the highest-yield neighborhoods, and vacancy, repairs and unpaid rent usually cost more there.

      Estimates only — not appraisals, and not financial, legal or tax advice. This page has no crime, tax-delinquency or property-condition data. "Multi-family" combines 2–4 unit buildings. Check every number with a local agent, inspector and lender before you buy.
